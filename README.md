# AI Playlist Copilot

AI Playlist Copilot is a product concept for helping listeners rediscover music in their personal Spotify libraries through natural-language intent.

## Problem

People often remember the feeling, activity, or listening context they want, but not the artist, title, or keywords needed by conventional search. The concept reframes retrieval around prompts such as mood, activity, and desired listening duration.

## My role

- Defined the product positioning and MVP scope
- Wrote the product requirements document
- Created the information architecture and user/task flows
- Developed UX copy and multiple rounds of low-fidelity wireframes
- Covered onboarding, Spotify authorization and library sync, intent input, playlist results, history, feedback, and error states

## Current status

This repository documents a product and interaction design concept. It does not contain a production Spotify integration or a launched application.
